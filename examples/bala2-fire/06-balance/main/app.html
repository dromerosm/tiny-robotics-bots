Tuning

Every number the firmware lets you change. Tap i to see what a number does. Type a value and press Enter. The robot keeps it inside its range.

A change lasts until the next restart. Save keeps the numbers of now in the robot, so it starts with them. Defaults deletes them and brings back the firmware's numbers. Both need the robot stopped. bp and servo are never saved.
